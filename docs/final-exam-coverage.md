# Final exam study-guide coverage review

Reviewed November 15, 2026 against the user-supplied, three-page `FinalExamStudyGuide26.pdf`. The original PDF is not included in the website or repository.

## Shared structure

All 14 conditions have substantive notes in the same 16 sections, rendered in this order:

1. Overview & pathophysiology
2. Clinical manifestations
3. Risk factors & prevention
4. Diagnostic tests
5. Medical management
6. Pharmacological therapies
7. Non-pharmacological therapies
8. Treatment side effects & complications
9. Nutrition
10. Nursing assessment
11. Nursing diagnoses
12. Nursing priorities & first action
13. Plan of care
14. Nursing management & interventions
15. Patient education
16. Evaluation

The guide's general requirements apply to every condition, in addition to its named highlights. All conditions address those requirements. Diagnostic tests remain separate from treatment; medical management remains separate from nursing interventions. All care plans now use Goal / Actions / Evaluate cards. Earlier goals are preserved verbatim, with actions and evaluation summarized from the existing dedicated sections. Video resources follow the 16 sections, then clarifications and reading references at the bottom.

## Changes from this review

- Standardized the care-plan layout for Parkinson's, MG, MS, ALS, meningitis, GBS and RA to match the seven newer pages, preserving every original goal and introductory note.
- Expanded the previously generic Parkinson's adjunct-medication discussion with representative classes, examples, purpose, monitoring and teaching. Added explicit impulse-control, sudden-sleep and interruption safety points.
- Added MG complement-inhibitor meningococcal precautions to treatment side effects, including residual risk despite vaccination/antibiotics and urgent assessment.
- Added a concise cervical/endometrial/ovarian treatment comparison under medical management; the combined topic retains its requested breast-first scope.
- Removed the outdated study-guide introduction saying pages were still being added.

New clinical additions have primary/authoritative references on their condition pages. The audit checked the full supplied guide's coverage and performed targeted current-guidance safety checks; it does not represent independent revalidation of every medical statement or of external videos/presentation PDFs. The faculty guide states the exam is not limited to the guide.

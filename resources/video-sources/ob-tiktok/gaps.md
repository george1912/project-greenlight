# Gaps, source notes, and content notes (version 2)

All 115 videos were verified live on 2026-11-20. The TikTok page for each one was loaded, and the id, creator and caption were read from the page's own data. Each objective has at most 2 videos. Videos were found through TikTok's public keyword ("discover") pages, about 1,700 keyword pages probed and 3,300 candidate videos scanned, plus many web searches. TikTok search and creator profile pages need sign-in from the build machine, so individual creators could not be browsed in full.

## Content notes

- **Promotion:** several educators link study guides or courses (NurseInTheMaking, Archer Nursing, SimpleNursing, Level Up RN, Nurse Sarah). Lactation creators invite paid consults, and nurse.utin's bio links a shop. No video in the pack is a paid ad (`isAd` was false on every page).
- **Possibly graphic or clinical imagery:** postpartum bleeding, PPH and fundal massage (dr.mikaela.rush, nursezabe, karathebabynurse, _nursevargas, pivotalbirth), perineal care (karathebabynurse), mastitis, forceps birth, Nexplanon removal, placental abruption, and an ROP eye exam (thenicudoc). The videos were not watched frame by frame.
- **Parent-facing framing** (facts fine, audience is parents): mama_nurse_tina, justbritni (safe sleep), nicustrongdoc, birthandbabyuniversity, milkguide, nora.mom.baby.nurse, balancedkidsmn, midwifemillar.
- **Caption caveats:** nurseinthemakingkristine's Rh/RhoGAM clip was removed in v2. RhoGAM timing now comes from that.nurse.who.knows (28 weeks and within 72 hours). The BUBBLE-HE highlight follows the site's own Homans-sign clarification.
- **Removed in v2:** 17 rows, listed with reasons in `removed_videos.csv`. Most were the third video on an objective. Also removed: one off-target row (postpartum preeclampsia under thromboembolism), one parent-facing clip that was replaced, and the mixed-topic general clip.

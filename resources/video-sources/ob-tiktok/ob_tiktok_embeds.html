Normal postpartum care

  • Fundus should be firm and midline, near the umbilicus after birth, then descend about 1 cm a day; a high, deviated fundus suggests a full bladder.
  • Lochia moves from rubra to serosa to alba. Report soaking a pad in an hour or less, large clots, or a foul odor.
  • BUBBLE-HE organizes the check: Breasts, Uterus, Bladder, Bowels, Lochia, Episiotomy/perineum, Homans/legs (check one leg for swelling, warmth, or pain; do not provoke Homans), Emotions.

  • The 4 Ts of PPH: Tone, Trauma, Tissue, Thrombin. Uterine atony is the most common cause.
